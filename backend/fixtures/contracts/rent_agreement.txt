RESIDENTIAL RENT AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 1 April 2025 between Sample Landlord Mehta (the "Landlord") and Sample Tenant Iyer (the "Tenant"). It is a sample written for testing and names no real person.

1. Premises and term
1.1 The Landlord lets to the Tenant the residential flat numbered 4B, Example Heights, Sample Nagar (the "Premises") for a term of eleven months from 1 April 2025.
1.2 The Premises shall be used only as a private residence.

2. Rent
2.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of Rs. 25,000 per month, on or before the 5th day of each month, by bank transfer.
2.2 Rent shall not be increased during the term.

3. Maintenance and utilities
3.1 The Tenant shall pay the electricity, water and gas charges for the Premises.
3.2 The Landlord shall pay the society maintenance charges and property tax.

4. Security deposit
4.1 On signing this agreement the Tenant shall pay the Landlord an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 1,50,000.
4.2 The Landlord shall refund the deposit within 30 days after the Tenant vacates the Premises, after deducting only the cost of damage beyond normal wear and tear and any unpaid rent.

5. Termination
5.1 Either party may end this agreement by giving the other two months' written notice.
5.2 The Tenant shall hand back the Premises in the condition in which they were received, allowing for normal wear and tear.

6. Repairs
6.1 The Tenant shall carry out minor repairs costing less than Rs. 1,000. The Landlord shall carry out all other repairs within a reasonable time.

7. Renewal
7.1 This agreement may be renewed for a further term on terms the parties agree in writing before it ends.
