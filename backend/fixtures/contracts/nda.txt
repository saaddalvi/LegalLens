MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 15 May 2025 between Sample Analytics Private Limited (the "Company") and Sample Consultant Desai (the "Consultant"). It is a sample written for testing and names no real person or company.

1. Purpose
1.1 The Company and the Consultant wish to discuss a possible consulting engagement on the Company's data-analysis project (the "Purpose"). Each may share confidential information with the other for the Purpose only.

2. Confidential information
2.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public business, technical or financial information that one party discloses to the other in writing or orally and that is marked or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is public without breach of this agreement, was already known to the receiving party, or is independently developed by it.

3. Obligations
3.1 The receiving party shall keep the Confidential Information secret, use it only for the Purpose and not copy it except as the Purpose requires.
3.2 The receiving party may share Confidential Information with its employees and advisers who need to know it and are bound to keep it confidential.
3.3 The receiving party may disclose Confidential Information where a court or authority requires it by law, after giving the other party prompt notice where permitted.

4. Duration
4.1 The obligations in this agreement continue for five years after the date of this agreement.

5. Return of information
5.1 When the other party asks, the receiving party shall return or destroy the Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so.

6. Breach
6.1 If the Consultant discloses Confidential Information in breach of this agreement, the Consultant shall pay the Company Rs. 50,00,000 as a penalty, whether or not the Company suffers any loss.

7. Restriction on other work
7.1 For three years after this agreement ends, the Consultant shall not carry on, or work for, any business similar to the Company's business anywhere in India.

8. General
8.1 This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject. It may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.
