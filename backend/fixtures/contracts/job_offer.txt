OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT

Date: 10 June 2025

Dear Sample Candidate Nair,

Northwind Sample Software Private Limited (the "Company") is pleased to offer you employment on the terms below. This letter is a sample written for testing and names no real person or company.

1. Position
1.1 You will join the Company as Software Engineer, reporting to the Engineering Manager, at the Company's office in Sample City.
1.2 Your date of joining will be 1 July 2025.

2. Salary
2.1 Your annual fixed compensation will be Rs. 12,00,000, paid in equal monthly instalments after deduction of tax at source.
2.2 You will be eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 10% of your fixed compensation at the Company's discretion.

3. Probation
3.1 You will be on probation for the first six months. The Company will confirm you in writing at the end of probation if your performance is satisfactory.

4. Working hours and leave
4.1 Your normal working hours are 9 hours a day, five days a week.
4.2 You will be entitled to 18 days of paid leave in each calendar year, plus public holidays.

5. Confidentiality
5.1 While you work for the Company and afterwards, you shall not disclose the Company's confidential information to anyone outside the Company.

6. Notice period
6.1 After confirmation, either you or the Company may end your employment by giving 60 days' written notice or salary in lieu of notice.
6.2 During probation the notice period is 15 days.

7. Intellectual property
7.1 Software and other work you create in the course of your employment belongs to the Company.

8. Post-employment restriction
8.1 For 24 months after your employment ends, for any reason, you shall not join, work for or provide services to any company that competes with the Company, anywhere in India.
8.2 If you break this restriction, you shall repay the Company all salary paid to you in your last 12 months of employment.

9. Acceptance
9.1 Please sign and return a copy of this letter within 7 days to accept this offer.
